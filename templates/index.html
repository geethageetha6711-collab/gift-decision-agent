        LangGraph Architecture

        Decision & Feedback Workflows

        START ↓
        1. Preference Validator

        Normalizes budget bounds, deduplicates interests, initializes shared state

        ↓
        2. Gift Candidate Generator

        Gemini AI structured synthesis or deterministic catalogue retrieval (5-8 candidates)

        ↓
        3. Constraint Filter

        Hard budget ceiling, excluded categories, already-owned items

        ↓ [Valid candidates ≥ 3] ↶ [Too few candidates → Regenerate]
        4. Deterministic Scoring Engine

        Normalized 100-point algorithm: Interest 30%, Budget 25%, Relevance 20%, Purpose 15%, Personalization 10%

        ↓
        5. Decision Reviewer

        Audits budget limit, exclusion violations, and category diversity (max 3 loops)

        ↓
        6. Final Recommendation Synthesis

        Constructs rationale, alternatives comparison, and ReportLab PDF payload

        ↓
        END
        START (User Rejection) ↓
        1. Feedback Analyzer

        Extracts rejection intent (e.g. "Too expensive", "Too generic", "More practical")

        ↓
        2. Preference & Constraint Updater

        Excludes rejected candidates, adapts budget ceilings, updates preference weights

        ↓
        3. Candidate Regenerator

        Generates fresh candidates respecting newly added exclusions

        ↓
        4. Constraint Filter & Scoring Engine

        Enforces hard bounds and recalculates transparent scores

        ↓
        5. Updated Recommendation

        Delivers adapted recommendation with explanation of what shifted

        ↓
        END
